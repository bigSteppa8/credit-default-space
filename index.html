Credit Card Default Predictor

Estimates the probability that a credit card client defaults on next month's payment, using a logistic regression model trained on the UCI “Default of Credit Card Clients” dataset (30,000 Taiwanese clients, April–September 2005). Amounts are in New Taiwan dollars (NT$). The model runs entirely in your browser.

    Coursework demo for AAI6610 (Module 8 logistic regression). Not for real lending decisions: the model is trained on 2005 data from one bank, misses about two thirds of actual defaulters at the 50% threshold, and uses sex, age and marital status, which would raise fairness and legal concerns in production.
